# TRIVX – Pharmaceutical & Healthcare

---

## Corporate Overview

TRIVX – Pharmaceutical & Healthcare is a UAE-based international healthcare trading and distribution company specializing in the sourcing and supply of pharmaceutical medicines, consumer healthcare products, supplements, baby care essentials, and medical equipment.

Operating from the United Arab Emirates, TRIVX serves as a strategic bridge between global healthcare manufacturers and regional and international markets, ensuring reliable access to quality-assured healthcare products across multiple categories.

The company is positioned as a multi-segment healthcare solutions provider, integrating pharmaceutical trade, consumer wellness products, and medical supply chain distribution under one unified platform.

---

## Business Model

TRIVX operates through a diversified healthcare supply framework focused on:

- Global pharmaceutical trading and distribution
- Consumer healthcare and wellness products
- Baby care and hygiene solutions
- Medical and hospital equipment supply

This structure enables TRIVX to serve hospitals, pharmacies, wholesalers, distributors, and institutional healthcare providers through a single integrated supply network.

---

## Core Business Activities

### 1. Pharmaceutical Trading & Global Distribution

TRIVX specializes in the sourcing and distribution of branded and generic pharmaceutical medicines from internationally recognized manufacturers.

Operations include:

- Import and export of regulated pharmaceutical products
- Distribution of essential and specialty medicines
- Supply to pharmacies, hospitals, and healthcare institutions
- Coordination with certified global pharmaceutical manufacturers

All pharmaceutical products are handled through compliant and structured supply channels, maintaining authenticity and reliability.

---

### 2. Healthcare Supplements & Wellness Products

TRIVX is actively involved in the distribution of health supplements and nutritional healthcare products, supporting preventive healthcare and daily wellness needs.

Product categories include:

- Vitamins and dietary supplements
- Nutritional health products
- Immunity support formulations
- General wellness healthcare solutions

All products are sourced from certified manufacturers ensuring safety, efficacy, and quality compliance.

---

### 3. Baby Care & Hygiene Products

TRIVX supplies a complete range of infant care and hygiene essentials, focusing on comfort, safety, and skin protection.

Product range includes:

- Baby milk and infant nutrition products
- Diapers and baby hygiene products
- Infant care essentials and related consumables

All products are selected based on quality standards, dermatological safety, and international certification compliance.

---

### 4. Medical, Surgical & Hospital Equipment

TRIVX provides supply solutions for medical and healthcare institutions, including essential hospital infrastructure and clinical equipment.

Product scope includes:

- Surgical instruments and consumables
- Hospital and clinical equipment
- Diagnostic and patient monitoring systems
- Emergency and critical care supplies

All products are sourced from approved and internationally certified manufacturers to meet healthcare facility requirements.
