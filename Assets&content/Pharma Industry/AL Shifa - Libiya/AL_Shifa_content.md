## Company Overview

Al-Shiffa Pharma is a registered pharmaceutical company in Libya, specializing in the import and distribution of medicines and medical supplies sourced from leading international and regional manufacturers.

The company is officially registered with relevant governmental authorities, including the Ministry of Health and Medical Supply, ensuring full compliance with national healthcare regulations.
