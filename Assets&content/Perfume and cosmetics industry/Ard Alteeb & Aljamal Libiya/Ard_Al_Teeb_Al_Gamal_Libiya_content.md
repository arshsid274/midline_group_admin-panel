# Ard Alteeba & AlGamal

---

## Company Overview

Founded in 2017, Ard Alteeba & AlGamal was established with a bold vision to lead, innovate, and redefine the beauty and fragrance industry in Libya.

Headquartered in Benghazi, the company has grown from a local business into a nationally recognized brand with a strong and expanding presence across the country.



---

## Growth & Market Presence

Ard Alteeba & AlGamal has developed a strong retail and distribution network across Libya.

### Key Highlights:
- Presence in all major cities across Libya  
- 150+ retail points nationwide  
- Strong distribution channels ensuring consistent availability  

This extensive reach positions the company as one of the most accessible and trusted names in the Libyan beauty market.
