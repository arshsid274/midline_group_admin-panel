# Almutakbal Alsheeh Ltd Company

---

## Company Overview

Almutakbal Alsheeh Ltd Company is a specialized automotive business in Libya, focused on the trade of modern auto spare parts and the delivery of comprehensive vehicle maintenance services.

The company operates across both wholesale and retail segments, while also managing a fully equipped service center dedicated to modern vehicles—offering end-to-end automotive solutions under one roof.

Established in Benghazi in 2020 with a single branch, the company has grown steadily through strong market trust and operational excellence. By 2026, Almutakbal Alsheeh proudly operates **5 branches in Benghazi**, reflecting its continuous expansion and success in the automotive sector.

With a commitment to quality and reliability, the company supplies genuine and high-quality spare parts for a wide range of vehicle brands, ensuring customer satisfaction and long-term performance.

---

## Corporate Affiliation

Almutakbal Alsheeh Ltd is a proud member of the **Midline Holding Group** — a diversified multi-sector conglomerate headquartered at Burlington Tower, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE.

Within the Group's corporate structure, Almutakbal Alsheeh operates under the **Spare Parts Division** alongside its UAE-based sister company, **ARC Auto Spare Parts Trading LLC**. Together, they form Midline's automotive supply and services arm, covering both the UAE and Libyan markets.

This Group backing provides Almutakbal Alsheeh with access to international sourcing networks, procurement expertise, and cross-market operational support — enabling it to deliver world-class automotive solutions to the Libyan market.
