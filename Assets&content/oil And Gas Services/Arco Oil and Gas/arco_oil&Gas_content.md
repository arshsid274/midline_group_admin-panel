Built on a foundation of technical expertise, international sourcing strength, and operational excellence, the company serves as a trusted partner to energy operators and industrial clients worldwide.

## Products & Trading Portfolio

ARCO operates as a diversified trading partner in the oil and gas sector, supplying high-performance industrial and oilfield equipment.

### Key Offerings:

- Complete drilling rig packages and systems  
- Wellhead assemblies and pressure control systems  
- Industrial compressors and gas handling equipment  
- Power generation systems (diesel and gas generators)  
- Pipes, tubes, and line pipe solutions  
- Industrial valves, actuators, and flow control systems  
- Pumps and fluid handling equipment  
- OCTG (casing, tubing, drill pipe)  
- Drilling tools and downhole equipment  
- Pressure control and well intervention systems  
- Flanges, fittings, and connection components  
- Oilfield instrumentation and spare parts  

All products are sourced through vetted global OEMs and certified suppliers, ensuring compliance with international standards and operational reliability.
