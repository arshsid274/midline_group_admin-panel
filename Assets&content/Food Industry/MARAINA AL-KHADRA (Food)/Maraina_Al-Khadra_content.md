# MARAINA AL-KHADRA – Natural Food for a Healthy Life

At MARAINA AL-KHADRA, we take pride in being a leading company in the food industry, specializing in grain milling and rice processing since 2022. We are committed to delivering high-quality products sourced from the finest grains and local crops, while preserving their natural nutritional value and authentic taste. In addition, we import premium wheat, malt, and bran flour to ensure a consistent supply of top-quality raw materials for our production and distribution.

## Location

Al-Nawagiya, next to Al-Intila Gate – Benghazi, Libya

## Our Vision

To become a leading company in the production of essential food products in the region by offering healthy, natural products that contribute to building a healthier society.

## Our Mission

- To provide high-quality staple foods derived from grains and rice
- To import and supply premium wheat, malt, and bran flour to meet market needs
- To adhere to the highest food safety and health standards
- To support local farmers and strengthen the national economy
- To continuously innovate in production and packaging processes
- To achieve customer satisfaction through quality and excellent service
