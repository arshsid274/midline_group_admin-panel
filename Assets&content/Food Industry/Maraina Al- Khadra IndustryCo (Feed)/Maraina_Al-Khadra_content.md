# MARAINA AL-KHADRA (Green Pastures)

**Website:** https://green.maraina.com.ly/

---

## Company Overview

MARAINA AL-KHADRA, widely recognized under the brand **Green Pastures**, is a registered company in Libya specializing in the animal feed industry—one of the most critical pillars of the livestock and poultry sectors.

The company operates with a clear understanding that nutrition forms the foundation of animal productivity, accounting for up to **70% of production costs** and contributing to nearly **90% of success** in animal rearing.

With this perspective, Green Pastures has positioned itself as a **science-driven and performance-oriented feed solutions provider**, focused on enhancing efficiency, profitability, and sustainability within the agricultural sector.

The animal feed industry plays a vital role in:
