# Al-Dabussia for bottling Mineral Water Company

## Company Overview

Established in 2018, Al-Dabussia for bottling Mineral Water Company has positioned itself as a trusted provider of premium-quality drinking water, committed to meeting and exceeding international standards of safety, purity, and excellence. From its inception, the company has focused on delivering a product that reflects both natural integrity and technological precision, ensuring consistent quality for consumers across all markets it serves.

---

## Vision & Commitment

Aldabussia strives to be a leading name in the mineral water industry, recognized for:

- Superior product quality
- Advanced production standards
- Strong consumer trust

The company's commitment is rooted in providing safe, refreshing, and naturally balanced mineral water, supporting healthy lifestyles while maintaining environmental and operational responsibility.

---

## Certifications & Compliance

To reinforce its dedication to quality and safety, Aldabussia operates under internationally recognized certifications:

- **ISO 9001 — Quality Management System**
  Ensuring consistent operational excellence and continuous improvement across all processes.
- **ISO 22000 — Food Safety Management System**
  Guaranteeing the highest standards of food safety throughout the entire production and supply chain.

In addition, all operations strictly adhere to:

- **GMP** (Good Manufacturing Practices)
- **HACCP** (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)

These frameworks ensure that every stage — from sourcing to distribution — is carefully monitored and controlled to eliminate risks and maintain product integrity.

## Natural Source & Product Quality

At the core of Aldabussia's success is its reliance on naturally sourced mineral water, characterized by a balanced mineral composition. This natural advantage provides:

- Exceptional purity
- Refreshing and consistent taste
- High safety standards

This unique combination gives the company a distinct competitive edge in the local market, making its products a preferred choice among consumers.

## Advanced Water Treatment Technologies

To ensure absolute safety and purity, Aldabussia integrates cutting-edge treatment systems into its production process:

- **Ultraviolet (UV) Sterilization**
  Eliminates harmful microorganisms without altering the natural composition of the water.
- **Ozone Sterilization**
  Provides an additional layer of disinfection, ensuring complete removal of contaminants.

These technologies work in synergy to deliver crystal-clear, safe, and high-quality drinking water.
