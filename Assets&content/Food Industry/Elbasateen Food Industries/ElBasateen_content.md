# Al Basateen Food Industries Company

## Company Overview
**Address:** Al-Talhiyah, Benghazi, Libya  

Al Basateen Food Industries Company, established in 2006 and headquartered in Benghazi, Libya, is a leading food manufacturing company committed to delivering high-quality, safe, and nutritious food products to the local market.

Strategically located in Benghazi – the Pharmaceutical Market area, near Al-Ahly Club, the company benefits from strong accessibility and proximity to key commercial and distribution hubs.

Since its inception, Al Basateen has focused on combining modern industrial practices with consistent quality standards, positioning itself as a trusted name in Libya’s food production sector.

---

## Strategic Development & Industrial Capability

As part of its continuous growth and modernization strategy, Al Basateen has invested in advanced production technologies and international partnerships.

A key milestone includes collaboration with **Osmanli Makina**, providing:

- Fully integrated tomato paste production line  
- End-to-end processing from fresh tomatoes to finished paste  
- Automation and efficiency across all production stages  

Additionally, the company has established a complete legumes canning production line, ensuring large-scale production with consistent quality and hygiene standards.

---

## Production Capacity & Operational Strength

Al Basateen operates with high-performance machinery capable of producing up to:

**30,000 cans per day**

This ensures:

- Continuous and reliable supply  
- Ability to meet growing consumer demand  
- Efficient large-scale production with consistent quality  

The factory is designed for high efficiency, minimal waste, and optimized output.
