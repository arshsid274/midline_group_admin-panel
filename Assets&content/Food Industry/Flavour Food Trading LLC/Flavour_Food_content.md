# Flavor Food Trading FZE — Corporate Overview

---

## About

Flavor Food Trading FZE is a UAE-based international trading and industrial solutions company engaged in the sourcing, distribution, and supply of raw materials, food commodities, finished food products, and complete food production line systems.

The company operates as an integrated partner to the global food industry, connecting world-renowned manufacturers, suppliers, and industrial technology providers with markets across the Middle East, Africa, Asia, and other international regions.

With a strong foundation in trade and industrial supply, Flavor Food Trading FZE has positioned itself as a reliable one-stop solution provider for food production and processing industries.

---

## Business Scope & Activity

The company's operations are structured across two core divisions:

---

### 1. Food Trading Division

This division focuses on the international sourcing and supply of high-quality food raw materials and finished products. The company ensures consistency in quality, competitive pricing, and dependable supply chains through long-term relationships with global producers.

#### Raw Materials & Ingredients

- Juice concentrates used in beverage and processing industries
- Food flavors and specialty ingredients
- Milk powder for dairy and food production applications
- Industrial-grade raw materials for food processing units

#### Agricultural Commodities

- Milling wheat and barley for flour and feed industries
- Rice in various grades and origins
- Sugar for industrial and retail applications
- Tea and coffee for FMCG and beverage markets

#### Finished Food Products

- Evaporated milk
- Infant milk / baby formula products
- Canned tuna and seafood products
- Edible corn oil and related food oils

---

### 2. Industrial & Production Line Division

Beyond trading, the company provides complete industrial solutions for food manufacturing facilities, supplying production line machinery and turnkey systems designed for efficiency, hygiene, and scalability.

This division supports investors and manufacturers in setting up or expanding food production facilities with full technical and commercial support.

#### Production Line Solutions Offered

**Oil Production Lines**
Complete systems for the extraction, refining, and processing of edible oils from raw materials such as seeds and vegetables. Designed for high efficiency, industrial-scale output, and international quality standards.

**Tomato Paste Production Lines**
Fully automated production systems covering washing, crushing, heating, concentration, and packaging processes. Designed for consistent quality and high-volume production.

**Pasta Production Lines**
Industrial pasta manufacturing systems capable of producing various pasta types including spaghetti, macaroni, and specialty shapes, with integrated drying and packaging technology.

**Chicken Slaughtering & Poultry Processing Lines**
Advanced hygienic processing systems for poultry slaughtering, cleaning, cutting, and packaging, designed in compliance with international food safety regulations.

**Tuna Production & Canning Lines**
Complete fish processing and canning systems including cleaning, cooking, filling, sealing, and sterilization processes for tuna and seafood products.
