# Al-Aws Libyan Company for Food Import

---

## Company Overview

Al-Aws Libyan Company for Food Import was established in Benghazi with a clear and ambitious vision to bridge the quality gap in the local market. Over the years, the company has grown to become one of the leading businesses in the import and distribution of food products across Libya.

With a capital of **6 million Libyan Dinars**, Al-Aws continues to strengthen its position by delivering high-quality products and reliable services to a wide range of customers.

---

## Vision

To be the leading and most preferred Libyan company in food import, and a trusted partner for restaurants, hotels, markets, and consumers seeking quality and excellence.

---

## Mission

To provide a wide range of high-quality imported food products at competitive prices, supported by exceptional customer service and a commitment to delivering products with the highest standards of safety and freshness. This is achieved through building long-term partnerships with global suppliers and developing an efficient logistics network.

---
