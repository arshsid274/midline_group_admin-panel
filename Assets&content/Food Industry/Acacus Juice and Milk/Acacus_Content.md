# Acacus Juice & Milk Factory

## Company Overview
**Address:** Al-Talhiyah, Benghazi, Libya  

Acacus Juice & Milk Factory stands as one of Libya’s most advanced and forward-looking industrial food manufacturing projects. Officially inaugurated on February 4, 2024, in Benghazi, the factory represents a significant milestone in the country’s journey toward industrial growth, food security, and self-sufficiency.

The establishment of Acacus reflects a broader national vision to strengthen the local food manufacturing sector, reduce reliance on imports, and provide consumers with high-quality, locally produced food and beverage products that meet international standards.

## Certifications & Quality Assurance

Acacus operates in strict compliance with internationally recognized standards:

### ISO Certifications:
- **ISO 22000:2018 – Food Safety Management System**  
  Ensures comprehensive control over food safety hazards throughout production  

- **ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management System**  
  Guarantees consistent product quality through standardized processes  

These certifications reflect a strong commitment to safety, reliability, and premium-quality production.

## Advanced Manufacturing Technologies

The factory is equipped with state-of-the-art systems:

- **UHT (Ultra-High Temperature) Pasteurization Systems**  
  Extends shelf life while preserving nutritional value  

- **High-Efficiency Filling & Packaging Lines**  
  Ensures precision, speed, and hygienic processing  

- **Automated Production Lines**  
  Minimizes human intervention for consistency and safety
