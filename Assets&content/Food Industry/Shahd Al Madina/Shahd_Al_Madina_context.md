# SHAHD AL MADINA

## Company Profile

### Corporate Overview

Shahd Al Madina is a UAE-based food manufacturing company specializing in the production of high-quality beverages and packaged food products for regional and international markets.

The company is engaged in the manufacturing of juices, flavored milk, evaporated milk, and canned tuna — delivering products that combine taste, nutrition, and consistent quality standards.

Shahd Al Madina operates with a strong commitment to food safety, product innovation, and reliable production processes, positioning itself as a growing player in the fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) sector.

---

## Business Focus

The company is primarily focused on the production and supply of:

- Fruit juices and juice-based beverages
- Flavored milk products
- Evaporated milk
- Canned tuna and seafood products

Each product category is developed with attention to taste consistency, nutritional value, and international quality standards.

---

## Manufacturing Capability

Shahd Al Madina operates with a structured manufacturing approach that ensures:

- Hygienic food production processes
- Controlled quality assurance systems
- Standardized production lines
- Efficient packaging and preservation techniques
- Compliance with food safety regulations

The company is designed to support scalable production and consistent output quality across all product categories.

## Quality & Food Safety Standards

The company places strong emphasis on maintaining high levels of food safety and quality assurance through:

- Strict hygiene protocols in production facilities
- Raw material quality inspection and control

- Compliance with international food safety requirements

This ensures that all products meet consumer safety expectations and regulatory standards.
