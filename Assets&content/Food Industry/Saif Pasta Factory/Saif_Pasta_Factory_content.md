Saif Pasta Factory
Company Overview
Saif Pasta Factory, headquartered in Benghazi, Libya, is one of the country’s most recognized and well-established food manufacturing facilities. Since its founding in 2006, the factory has consistently delivered high-quality pasta products, earning a strong reputation for reliability, consistency, and customer satisfaction.
With nearly two decades of industry experience, Saif Pasta Factory has grown into a key contributor to Libya’s food manufacturing sector, supporting local demand while strengthening the country’s move toward self-sufficiency in essential food products.
 
Industry Position & Contribution
Saif Pasta Factory plays a vital role in:
• Supporting the local food supply chain
• Reducing dependence on imported pasta products
• Contributing to economic development and industrial growth
• Providing consistent, affordable, and high-quality food options
With a production capacity of 17.5 tons per day, the factory ensures a steady and reliable supply to meet the needs of the local market.
 
Production Capacity & Operational Strength
The factory is designed for efficient, large-scale production, enabling it to:
• Maintain continuous output to meet market demand
• Ensure timely distribution across various regions
• Adapt to increasing consumption needs
Its scalable infrastructure positions the company for future expansion and increased production capabilities.
